How a project runs

1

  1. Tell us what you need

    Send a WhatsApp message with what you want made, your deadline, and any logo, photos or text you already have.

  2. We agree the idea and the price

    We reply with a quote and a direction for the design before any work starts.

  3. We design, you review

    You get a first version to look at. Tell us what to change and we refine it.

  4. You get your files

    Final artwork arrives ready for print, screen or social media, in the formats you need.
