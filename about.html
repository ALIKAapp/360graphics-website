One studio. Every side of your brand.

360 Graphics is a graphic design company in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania. We have been designing for businesses, events and organisations since 2017.

Why 360

A brand is never seen from one side only. The same business shows up on a poster by the road, on a phone screen, on a product label and on the radio.

360 Graphics exists to make all of those look and sound like one company. The studio opened in 2017. Since then we have delivered more than 5,000 projects, from single business cards to complete brand systems, motion graphic adverts and voice-over adverts.

Clients come to us for one poster and stay for the logo, the packaging, the video and the website, because keeping everything with one team keeps the brand consistent.

What we hold ourselves to

Design that sells, not just decorates

Every piece has a job: get a call, fill an event, move a product off the shelf. We design for that result first and make it beautiful second.

Words that sound like your customers

Scripts and voice-overs are written the way your customers actually speak, in English or Swahili, never translated word for word.

Files that work everywhere

You receive artwork that is correctly sized and ready for the printer, the screen and social media, so you never have to come back for another format.

One team from logo to billboard

The people who design your logo also make your poster, your video advert and your website, so nothing drifts off-brand.
